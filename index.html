          About this page

          We counted the candy on Nov. 1, 2026 and typed it into a spreadsheet: one row for each kind of candy, one column for each kid. The Girl is 4 and went as a Rainbow Fairy with Mom and Dad. The Boy is 9, went as Colonel Sanders, and trick-or-treated with friends.

          Calorie numbers are rough guesses for a typical fun-size piece. How we grouped the candy (chocolate, gummy, hard candy) is our own call.
